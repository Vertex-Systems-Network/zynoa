# ADR-013 — Credits as internal currency with reservation protocol

Date: decided in the build spec v2.0 (11 Oct 2026); recorded 2026-10-15 · Status: accepted · Source: `docs/spec/17_DECISIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §1

## Decision

Credits as internal currency with reservation protocol.

## Why

Predictable margins over volatile provider prices; budgets.

## Alternatives rejected

Pass-through provider billing; unlimited plans.
