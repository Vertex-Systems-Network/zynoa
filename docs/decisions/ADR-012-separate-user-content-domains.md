# ADR-012 — Previews/user content on separate registrable domains

Date: decided in the build spec v2.0 (11 Oct 2026); recorded 2026-10-15 · Status: accepted · Source: `docs/spec/17_DECISIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §1

## Decision

Previews/user content on separate registrable domains.

## Why

Prevents cookie/session theft and phishing on app domain.

## Alternatives rejected

Subdomains of app domain.
