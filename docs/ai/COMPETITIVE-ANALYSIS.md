## Where Zynoa must win (superiority claim)

No single-lane superiority is claimed (EVID-000001). The bet (spec §6 "honest risk", EVID-000002): the **combination** — one project
graph, verified progress, ready Business Systems, and local market fit (WhatsApp reminders, PKR billing,
Urdu public pages later) — beats each lane specialist for one beachhead segment. Segment choice is OD-01
(recommendation: developers/small agencies), to be confirmed by Phase 0 interviews.
